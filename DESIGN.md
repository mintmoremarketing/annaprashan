# Smriti's Annaprashan — visual system

The site is a quiet editorial interpretation of the family's printed invitation. It uses their ivory, blush, rose, and gold palette and the supplied illustration. The scratch panel is the one playful moment; the rest of the page is composed like a carefully typeset invitation.

## Palette

- Paper `#f8f1ec`; deeper paper `#efe4dd`
- Ink `#463d39`; secondary ink `#6f625e`
- Rose `#b96e7b`; deeper rose `#934f5d`
- Blush `#e6c7c7`; warm gold `#bb9563`

## Typography

- Cormorant Garamond for display and personal messages.
- DM Sans for details, navigation, and actions.
- Noto Serif Bengali for Bengali script.

## Layout and interaction

- The desktop opening pairs the invitation copy with a crop of the supplied card artwork. Mobile stacks these elements.
- The date panel has a pointer driven scratch surface and a separate button so guests can reveal it with keyboard or assistive technology.
- Venue details remain plain text with a direct Google Maps link.
- The first visit opens with a kheer bowl: a circular stirring gesture fills its progress ring and opens the invitation. A visible bypass and keyboard path remain available.
- The hero briefly holds while scrolling, moving its type and portrait at different rates before the date section arrives. Mobile uses the same scroll relationship without pinning the tall content.
- Supporting motion includes restrained scroll entrances, a gently responsive portrait, drifting hero leaves, a blessing card that lifts and releases petals, and a small petal celebration when the date is revealed. Reduced motion preferences are honored.

## Content constraints

The card supplies no event time. Do not invent one. The user explicitly removed RSVP.

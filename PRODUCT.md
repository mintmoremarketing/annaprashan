# Smriti's Annaprashan invitation

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, confirmed by the user for a shareable invitation.

## Users

Family and invited guests opening an invitation link, primarily on their phones.

## Product Purpose

Invite guests to Smriti Biswas's Annaprashan and make the date, family, venue, and directions easy to find.

## Operating Context

The invitation celebrates Smriti Biswas, daughter of Sourav Biswas and Sromona Biswas. The event is Saturday, 26 December 2026, when Smriti is six months old, at Parish Hall, St. Paul's Cathedral, Rabindra Sadan, 1A Cathedral Road, Maidan, New Market Area, Kolkata, West Bengal 700071.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- A tactile scratch interaction reveals the date, with an accessible reveal button.
- The user explicitly requested no RSVP.
- The card does not provide an event time, so the site must not invent one.
- Map directions should open the supplied venue location.

## Brand Commitments

Use the supplied invitation card's ivory, blush pink, muted rose, and warm gold palette. The user chose a quiet editorial style with neat alignment and an Indian cultural feel.

## Evidence on Hand

The user supplied the invitation card image, which contains the names, event, date, and venue. The depicted baby is part of that card artwork; no separate photograph was supplied.

## Product Principles

- Let guests understand the invitation and essential details quickly.
- Keep interactions meaningful and easy to bypass.
- Preserve the family's supplied wording and event facts.
